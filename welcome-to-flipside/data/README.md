### Important Things to Know

* **AI-First Approach**: With Flipside AI, you can now analyze blockchain data using natural language prompts ("vibecoding") instead of writing SQL code. Our AI agents run 24/7 research across the entire crypto ecosystem.
* **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**: Flipside AI integrates directly with leading AI platforms through MCP, enabling instant blockchain intelligence in your preferred AI tools. [See our MCP Getting Started Guide](https://docs.flipsidecrypto.xyz/welcome-to-flipside/flipside-growth-mcp/anthropic-flipside-mcp-getting-started-guide)

* Our data structures are designed for ease of use and efficient querying, and are extensively documented. We regularly update our data structures to optimize efficiency.
* Latency varies from table to table, but typically lags either 1 hour or 1 day at most. Here's our [latency targets for each blockchain](https://docs.flipsidecrypto.xyz/data/flipside-data/table-freshness-targets).
